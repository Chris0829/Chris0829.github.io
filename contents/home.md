I am currently a Master's student in Information and Communication Engineering at the **Hangzhou Institute of Technology, Xidian University**. My research interests are **graph neural networks**, **spatiotemporal data mining**, and **multimodal recommender systems**. In my research, I mine the graph-structure information in user–item recommendation scenarios to improve recommendation accuracy and model efficiency. In addition, I maintain a strong research passion for **urban structure & climate resilience**, quantifying how street-network topology embeds urban thermal inequity. I aspire to apply graph-structure information theory and methods to this field and dedicate myself to addressing future global climate change and disaster response.
